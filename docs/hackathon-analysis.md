# Hackathon reconnaissance

Checked 3 October 2026. Sources are the current official pages, not the 2025 event.

## Mandatory submission requirements

The [2026 rules](https://paypalaihackathon.devpost.com/rules) require a functional application combining PayPal Sandbox with AI; PayPal must be central. Provide an English description, a public GitHub repository with a detectable open-source license, and working judge access. Hosted deployment is optional if complete run instructions suffice. The video must be public on YouTube and shorter than three minutes. Keep judge access available through judging. Solo individuals may enter, subject to eligibility restrictions. New projects qualify; existing projects require significant updates during the event.

Deadline: **12 November 2026, 12:00 PST / 21:00 Europe/Madrid**. Judging runs 1–15 December; winners are expected 21 December.

The five equally weighted criteria are technical implementation, design, potential impact, innovation and presentation. Stage one checks viability and required platform use. MIT meets the open-source requirement. Ownership remains with the entrant, with judging and promotional permissions in the rules. Check personal eligibility and third-party asset rights before submission. One main prize and one sponsor prize are the maximum combination.

## Zero-cost AI verification

The [live model catalog](https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/models) was fetched during implementation. `google/gemma-4-31b-it:free` exists (the prompt's slug without `-it` does not). Prompt and completion prices are zero and it lists tool use and response-format support. Its available free endpoint returned HTTP 404 for the required strict JSON-schema parameters. The tested primary is therefore `nvidia/nemotron-3-super-120b-a12b:free`, which successfully parsed the mission and planned actual Channel3 research. No universal benchmark superiority is claimed.

Also verified: `qwen/qwen3.8-27b:free` and `nvidia/nemotron-3-super-120b-a12b:free` list zero pricing and structured-output support. They are explicit allowlisted alternatives; Nemotron is the current primary. Default fallback is `openrouter/free`. [Free routing](https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/routing/model-variants/free) is subject to availability and rate limits. [Structured output](https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/features/structured-outputs) requires compatible endpoints. Every provider instance checks catalog prices before sending inference, enforces zero maximum provider prices, and validates output with Zod. Unavailable strict output produces a bounded free fallback or an error, never a paid request.

## Deliverables and risks

Repository, deployment, README, Devpost copy, screenshot pack and a **2:50** script are included. Recording and public YouTube upload remain human tasks. Final judging evidence must use real AI and a real Sandbox capture; local fixture screenshots are explicitly development assets.

[Channel3's documented offers](https://docs.trychannel3.com/guides/response-overview) contain retailer prices and availability, but do not guarantee ratings, committed delivery, exact stock quantities or taxes/shipping. Unknown evidence blocks policy. An explicit user-reviewed merchant quote can fill the missing fields; its provenance stays labeled as user attestation. No goods are fulfilled by the configured PayPal Sandbox test merchant.

Free models may rate-limit; sponsor credits may run out; stateless capabilities expire; production lacks global durable revocation and a tamper-proof audit ledger. These limits are disclosed. Recheck all rules and sponsor terms shortly before submitting.
