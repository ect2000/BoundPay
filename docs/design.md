# BoundPay design direction

Visual thesis: graphite surfaces, precise typography, and quiet blue light make financial authority feel tangible.

Content plan: full-canvas landing with a policy boundary diagram; focused mission input; comparison workspace with a persistent policy inspector; explicit payment mandate; traceable receipt.

Interaction thesis: flow nodes reveal in sequence, route surfaces transition quickly, and actual streamed research events populate the trace. Policy checks animate only after the deterministic evaluation completes. Drawers preserve context. Reduced motion disables movement.
